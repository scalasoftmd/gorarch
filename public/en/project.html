  • Rotate: Click and drag with the left mouse button or tap and drag on touch devices to rotate the model.
  • Zoom: Use the mouse wheel or pinch on touchscreens to zoom in and out.
  • Pan: Hold the right mouse button or use two fingers on a touchscreen and drag to pan the view.
  • Right Sidebar (Folded by Default): Click the arrow on the right to expand this panel. It contains:
    • Compare: View and compare different versions or states of your model side-by-side.
    • Presentation: Enter presentation mode to create guided views or walkthroughs.
    • Scene Settings: Adjust lighting, background, and rendering options for the model.
    • Selection Tools: Use Polygon, Point, or Object selection modes to highlight and adjust specific parts of the model.
  • Left Sidebar Workspace Button: Click this button to open the workspace panel. Here you can:
    • Layers: See all model layers, including point clouds, meshes, and orthophotos.
    • Add Layer: Upload additional layers or data files to overlay on the model.
    • View Layer: Toggle visibility of each layer to focus on specific data.
    • Hide Layer: Temporarily hide unwanted layers.
    • Zoom Layer: Focus the camera on a particular layer.
  • Fullscreen: Use the fullscreen button in the toolbar to view the model in fullscreen mode.
  • Additional Tools: Look out for measurement tools, annotations, or info pop-ups in the toolbar for extra interactivity.
