About Us

We transform ideas into works of art.

GOR Architecture is an architecture firm specializing in the design of residential, public, and industrial buildings. We combine creativity, engineering precision, and attention to detail to create modern and functional spaces.

Our team consists of experienced architects, engineers, and designers who manage projects through all stages — from concept to implementation. We value a personalized approach and always strive for each project to meet the client’s wishes and quality standards.

Our Services

Residential Building Design

We develop projects for private houses, row houses, and residential complexes — considering comfort, functionality, and aesthetics.

Public Building Design

We create projects for offices, schools, kindergartens, medical centers, and commercial spaces with efficient logistics and modern design.

Industrial Facility Design

We plan and design production spaces, warehouses, and logistics centers in compliance with technical standards and safety requirements.

Architectural Design
and Technical Documentation

We offer a complete package of plans and technical documentation needed for construction and permits.

3D Visualization and Facade Design

We create photorealistic visualizations of buildings and propose harmonious solutions for their exterior appearance.
